The moment leaders are in

Space to think, when everything is moving at once.

AI, uncertainty and rising expectations are reshaping how organisations work faster than most can absorb. Under pressure, leaders tend to either freeze or over-function — and judgement suffers either way.

Coaching interrupts that. It's a confidential, non-judgmental space to reflect, be challenged, and reconnect with what matters — so leaders stay grounded enough to be present today, and brave enough to shape what comes next.

Coaching with Alareo supports you to:

  • Have genuine space to think, explore and be challenged
  • Gain clarity and a clearer sense of direction
  • Build confidence and presence
  • Make better decisions under pressure
  • Navigate life transitions — a change in role, circumstances or stage
  • Be the best version of yourself, and deliver results

About

Aideen Cardiff

Founder & Executive Coach

Aideen brings more than three decades of senior leadership across global technology organisations — HP, IBM and Microsoft — spanning sales, consulting, partner ecosystems and large-scale transformation across Ireland, the UK and Europe.

That commercial credibility sits alongside a deep commitment to culture, inclusion and governance. She is a serving Board Chair and Non-Executive Director, and an accredited executive coach supporting leaders through uncertainty, change and transition — with a focus on confidence, clarity, decision-making, presence and wellbeing.

  • Executive Coaching Diploma, UCD Michael Smurfit (2025–26)
  • Chair, Work Equal · NED & ARC, St James's Hospital
  • M.Sc. in Business, UCC — Strategy, Innovation & OD
  • Governance & Compliance; Sustainability Leadership
  • Named a most influential Channel leader in Europe (CRN, 2020)
  • Qualified Organisational Development practitioner
